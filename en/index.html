How it works

One email. Three steps.

  1. You send a product.

    The name, the specs or model, a rough quantity and the city it goes to. One email is enough.

  2. We find and check suppliers.

    We shortlist makers abroad, check their export history and certificates, and get each one's quote in writing.

  3. You get the report.

    Suppliers side by side, the Saudi rules, HS code, duty, freight and an estimated landed cost. You decide, and buy direct.

How we work

Plain rules.

You stay the importer.

You buy from the supplier and pay them directly. We never buy, hold or import goods, and we never touch your money.

A fee, not a commission.

We're paid a fee for the report and take nothing from the supplier. Our only interest is the right fit for you.

Prices are the suppliers' own.

Every price in a report is a supplier's written quote and needs their confirmation. We never make up a price, a rule or a certificate.

Questions

Straight answers.

Who are you?

ARAQA is a new sourcing desk for Saudi companies, based in Riyadh and run by Yousif Shafie. You tell us a product you need; we find and check suppliers abroad and send back a report in Arabic and English. We do the research with AI tools, and every report is checked before it reaches you.

Who have you worked with?

No one yet. We're new, which is why our first client gets a two-week pilot paid on results only. We're finishing a sample report on a real product and can send it when it's ready.

How much does it cost?

We confirm the fee in writing before any work starts. Our first client's two-week pilot is paid on results only.

Can you buy, import or pay the supplier for me?

No. You're the importer and you pay the supplier directly. We never handle the goods or the money for them. We're paid only our fee.

Are the prices final? Do you guarantee the supplier?

The prices in a report are the suppliers' own quotes and need each supplier's written confirmation. We check each supplier's export history and certificates, but we don't guarantee the goods.

Do you clear customs or ship the goods?

No. The report tells you the rules, the documents and the costs. Your forwarder or customs broker handles shipping and clearance.

Which countries do you source from?

Wherever the right maker is. Mostly China, Turkey and India, sometimes the US and the Gulf.

Can you find a supplier in China, Turkey or India for my company in Saudi Arabia?

Yes, that is the whole job. Send the product, and we shortlist makers abroad, check their export history and certificates, and collect written quotes you can compare side by side.

Do you get the SABER certificate or SFDA approval for me?

No. The report tells you which rules apply to your product (SABER, SFDA, Civil Defense) and links to the official source. The certificate itself is issued through the official platforms by approved bodies, usually using the supplier's test reports.
